Credit Card Default Risk Predictor

Tuned random forest (100 trees, max depth 10, min 5 samples per leaf, balanced class weights) trained on the UCI Default of Credit Card Clients dataset (Yeh, 2009). Enter a customer's details, or load an example. The model runs entirely in your browser.

For coursework demonstration only (AAI6610, Northeastern University), not for real credit decisions. Data: Yeh, I.-C. (2009). Default of Credit Card Clients [Dataset]. UCI Machine Learning Repository. https://doi.org/10.24432/C55S3H
